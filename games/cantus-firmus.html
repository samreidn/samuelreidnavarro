      How to enter and edit

      Pick a note value, then click on the staff where the note should go: between two notes inserts it there, after the last note adds it at the end. Click a note to turn it into a rest of the same value; click a rest to remove it. Drag a note up or down to change its pitch. Click the clef to change it.

      To change a note without removing it, select it: right-click it, Shift-click it, or press and hold it on a touch screen. Value, rest and accidental buttons then change that note. With nothing selected, they apply to the next note you enter and to the note you just entered.

      A to GAdd that note, in the octave closest to the previous note
      RAdd a rest
      1 to 7Maxima, longa, brevis, semibrevis, minima, semiminima, fusa
      ↑ ↓Move the note a step up or down; with Shift, an octave
      ← →Select the previous or next note
      - N =Flat, natural, sharp (press again to remove)
      BackspaceDelete the selected note, or the one before the cursor
      EscDeselect
      SpacePlay or stop
      Ctrl/⌘ ZUndo; add Shift to redo
