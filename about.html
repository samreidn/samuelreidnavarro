Samuel Reid Navarro, born 1997 in Almere, the Netherlands. Studied music education at the Waldorf Institute in Leiden, choral conducting at the HKU of Utrecht, and music theory at the Schola Cantorum Basiliensis under Johannes Menke. Currently, he teaches in Graz at the Kunstuniversität Graz, theory of early music. In Basel, he continues studying singing in the medieval department of Schola Cantorum, with a focus on Renaissance and medieval polyphony and improvisation.
